# GTM Engineering Skills
Three Claude Skills for revenue teams — a small, connected toolkit for the work that usually happens by gut: figuring out who to target, deciding who's worth your time, and keeping the right accounts warm.

They're built to work as one loop:
1. **icp-definer** — sit down with Claude and define your Ideal Customer Profile through a guided interview. Out comes a clean, written ICP.
2. **gtm-qualification** — score a single lead or a whole list of accounts against that ICP. Out comes a ranked call list.
3. **account-nurture** — build a personalized, well-researched touch for a named account. Out comes a message you edit and send.

Define who you want → score who fits → nurture who you keep. Use them together or on their own.

## Install (about a minute each)
You'll need **code execution and file creation** turned on in Claude first: Settings → Capabilities. Then, for each skill:

1. Download the skill's ZIP from the [Releases](../../releases) page (or the folders below).
2. In Claude, go to **Customize → Skills**.
3. Click **+**, then **Create skill**, then **Upload a skill**.
4. Upload the ZIP. Toggle it on.

That's it. Trigger a skill by just asking — "help me define my ICP," "score these accounts," "build a nurture touch for [account]."

## Make them yours
These ship generic and work on install. To tune them to your company, product, and market, see **[CUSTOMIZE.md](CUSTOMIZE.md)** — including a paste-in prompt that has Claude interview you and rewrite the config in about five minutes.

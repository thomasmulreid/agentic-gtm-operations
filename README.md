# Agentic GTM Operations

Four Claude Skills for revenue teams — a connected toolkit for the work that usually happens by gut: figuring out who to target, deciding who's worth your time, keeping the right accounts warm, and pressure-testing the deals that matter.

Use them together as a loop, or install just the one you need. Each works on its own.

## The loop

1. **ICP Definer** — define your Ideal Customer Profile through a guided interview with Claude. Out comes a clean, written ICP.
2. **GTM Qualifier** — score a single lead or a whole list of accounts against that ICP. Out comes a ranked call list. *(Sharper if you feed it the doc from the ICP Definer, but works standalone.)*
3. **Account & Contact Nurture** — build a personalized, well-researched touch for a named account. Out comes a message you edit and send. For account managers and CSMs.
4. **Deal Coach** — review a live deal through hygiene, MEDDICC, stakeholders, and next moves. Out comes a read on where the deal is at risk and what to do this week.

Define who you want → qualify who fits → nurture who you keep → coach the deals that go live. Each stage stands on its own; together they cover the cycle.

## Get one skill, or all of them

Every skill is a separate download — take one or take the set.

1. Download the ZIP for the skill(s) you want from this repo (or the [Releases](../../releases) page).
2. In Claude, make sure **code execution and file creation** is on: Settings → Capabilities.
3. Go to **Customize → Skills → + → Create skill → Upload a skill**, and upload the ZIP.
4. Toggle it on. Trigger it by just asking — "help me define my ICP," "score these accounts," "build a nurture touch for [account]," "review this deal."

Repeat per skill if you want more than one.

## Make them yours

These ship generic and work on install. To tune them to your company, product, and market — including a paste-in prompt that has Claude interview you and rewrite the config in a few minutes — see **[CUSTOMIZE.md](CUSTOMIZE.md)**.

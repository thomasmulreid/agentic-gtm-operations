# Deal Coach — a Claude Skill

Take a live deal apart and find the thing that's quietly going to lose it. Paste your deal notes, a call summary, or just talk the deal through — you get back where the risk is, where the deal is thinner than its stage implies, and the specific moves to fix it.

## What it does

Runs an opportunity through four connected lenses:

- **Hygiene** — is the record honest? Right stage, real close date, multi-threaded, actually moving.
- **MEDDICC** — the qualification spine. Metrics, Economic buyer, Decision criteria, Decision process, Identify pain, Champion, Competition — each scored known / thin / gap, so the holes show.
- **Stakeholders** — who matters, where they lean, and whether you're resting the whole deal on one relationship.
- **Strategy** — the ranked moves that fall out of the gaps, biggest risk first, each tied to something the review actually found.

For enterprise or procurement-heavy deals, it also checks the **paper process** (the legal/procurement gauntlet after "yes") — the element that turns MEDDICC into MEDDPICC. It brings this in when the deal warrants it, rather than cluttering every review.

## The point

Most deal reviews confirm what you already believe. This one is built to find the gap you're talking past — the unnamed economic buyer, the "champion" who's really just friendly, the close date with no decision process behind it. **The gaps are the output.** It won't invent a champion you don't have to make the deal look better; a review that flatters you is worthless in the deal itself.

## How it runs

Three moves. First it **pulls** from whatever's connected — your CRM, email, call notes — plus anything you paste. Then it gives you a **fast first-pass review** off what it found, with every gap named out loud. Then it **challenges you** on those gaps and on the soft spots — the "champion" with no evidence, the ignored do-nothing competitor — asking the questions that turn a report into a real deal review. A well-documented deal gets a quick challenge round; a thin one gets a thorough one.

## What it needs

Nothing required. It works from what you paste or tell it. If you've got a CRM, email, or call-notes tool connected, it'll use them first and go deeper — and it'll tell you honestly what it couldn't assess when the data isn't there.

## What it won't do

- It won't update your CRM or send anything. It's a read, not an action — you keep the moves.
- It won't rubber-stamp a soft deal. If it's thinner than its stage, it says so.
- It won't fabricate deal facts. An unknown stays a gap, and the gaps are the to-do list.

## Where it fits

Part of the Agentic GTM Operations library. It picks up where qualification leaves off: once an account is qualified and becomes a live deal, this is what pressure-tests it.
